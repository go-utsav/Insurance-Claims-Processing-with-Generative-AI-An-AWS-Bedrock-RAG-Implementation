AUTO INSURANCE POLICY #998877
INSURER: SwiftCover Auto
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-01

SECTION A: COVERAGE LIMITS
1. Collision Coverage: Pays for damage to your vehicle caused by collision with another object or vehicle. 
   - Deductible: $500. 
   - Maximum Payout: Market value of the vehicle.
   - Requirement: A police report is MANDATORY for all collision claims over $2,000.

2. Comprehensive Coverage: Pays for damage caused by non-collision events (weather, theft, vandalism).
   - Deductible: $250.
   - Windshield Repair: $0 Deductible (Free).

3. Rental Reimbursement:
   - Covers rental car costs up to $40/day for a maximum of 30 days while the vehicle is being repaired.
   - This coverage only applies if the vehicle is not drivable.

SECTION B: FILING REQUIREMENTS
1. Timeline: All claims must be filed within 14 days of the incident.
2. Exclusions: Coverage is denied if the driver was using the vehicle for commercial purposes (e.g., rideshare, delivery) at the time of the incident without a commercial endorsement.
