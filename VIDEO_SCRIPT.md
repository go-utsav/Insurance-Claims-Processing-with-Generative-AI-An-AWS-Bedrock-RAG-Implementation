# YouTube Video Script — Auto Insurance Claim Check (FNOL)

**Style:** Simple, funny, non-tech. Use when you demo the app.

---

## Opening (30 sec)

**[LOW PITCH — calm, like you're telling a story]**  
"So you crash your car. You're stressed. You take a photo, you fill a form … and then you wait. And wait. *When* will someone tell you if you're covered?"

**[HIGH PITCH — excited]**  
"What if, instead of waiting 24 hours, you get an answer in seconds? That's what we're building today — a **data-driven** claim checker. You give it the claim; it compares it to the policy and tells you: approved, denied, or 'we need to look at this.'"

**[NORMAL]**  
"And yes — we're doing it the way AWS people talk about: **scalable**, **secure**, and aligned with the **Well-Architected Framework**. I'll keep it simple so you actually enjoy this."

---

## The Buzzwords (20 sec)

**[LOW PITCH — like a serious slide]**  
"Quick lingo so you sound like an AWS architect at a party: **Data-driven** means we use the claim data and the policy to decide — not guesswork. **Scalable** means when we move this to the cloud, it can handle way more claims without falling over. And the **Well-Architected Framework**? It's AWS's checklist: reliability, security, cost, performance. We're touching all of that."

**[HIGH PITCH]**  
"So when someone says 'is this production-ready?' — you can say: we're thinking reliability, we're thinking security. We're *well-architected*."

---

## The Demo (1 min)

**[NORMAL]**  
"Here's the app. Two columns: **Claim** on the left, **Result** on the right."

**[LOW PITCH]**  
"You can paste a claim, or use the form and even upload a police report or a crash photo. That file goes to **S3** — AWS's storage — so we keep everything in one place."

**[HIGH PITCH]**  
"You hit 'Compare against policy.' A few seconds later — boom. **Approved**, **Denied**, or **Review**. Plus we show you *why*: 'You filed 13 months late' or 'No police report for a big collision.' That's the AI reading the policy and your claim. **Document verification** checks dates and required docs so we're not just trusting the form — we're checking it."

**[NORMAL]**  
"So: data in, rules + AI, instant outcome. That's the FNOL dream — **First Notice of Loss** — without the long wait."

---

## Closing (15 sec)

**[LOW PITCH]**  
"Recap: we're **data-driven**, we're building for **scale**, and we're playing nice with **Well-Architected** — reliability, security, cost. No jargon overload, just a claim checker that could actually help someone on a bad day."

**[HIGH PITCH]**  
"If you liked this, smash that like, and I'll do more AWS + Gen AI stuff in plain English. See you in the next one."

---

## Keyword cheat sheet (for your own reference)

| Word / phrase            | Use it when…                          |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Data-driven**          | Talking about using claim + policy to decide |
| **Scalable**             | Talking about moving to cloud / more users   |
| **Well-Architected**     | Talking about reliability, security, design  |
| **S3**                   | Showing file upload / storage               |
| **Document verification**| Showing the checks (dates, police report)   |
| **FNOL**                 | First Notice of Loss — the “instant answer”  |

---

*Total speaking time: ~2 minutes. Adjust pace and add pauses when you click through the demo.*
